# EduHeal 🏥✨

> Bridging the gap between school health literacy and healthcare access through gamification, secure telemedicine, and predictive data science.

EduHeal is an intelligent, full-stack platform designed to tackle preventable school absenteeism at its root cause in underserved, rural, and semi-urban communities. 

---

## 🎯 The Problem & The Solution

### The Problem
Underprivileged students frequently lack access to basic health literacy and reliable healthcare. Preventable illnesses often cause high school absenteeism, leading to learning gaps and creating a cycle where poor health directly hinders educational success.

### The Solution
EduHeal breaks this cycle by partnering with schools to provide a mobile-first application that:
* **Gamifies Health Education:** Students complete interactive micro-lessons on hygiene, sanitation, and nutrition to earn **Health Points**.
* **Removes Economic Barriers:** Earned points are converted into **Consultation Tokens**, allowing parents to book free, in-app telemedicine appointments with volunteer healthcare professionals.
* **Deploys Preventive Care:** Builds proactive healthy habits directly within the student community.

---

## 🏗️ Core System Architecture

EduHeal utilizes a dual-database architecture optimized to handle both transactional accuracy and real-time frontend responsiveness.
